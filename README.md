# 🌟 My Favorite Movie: Superman (1978)

## 🎬 Overview
**Superman (1978)** is one of the most iconic superhero films ever made.  
Directed by *Richard Donner* and starring *Christopher Reeve*, it redefined the superhero genre with its inspiring story, groundbreaking effects, and unforgettable music.

---

## 🦸‍♂️ Why I Love This Movie
- Christopher Reeve’s performance is legendary  
- The story balances hope, heroism, and heart  
- The John Williams theme is one of the greatest movie scores ever  
- It captures the true spirit of Superman
